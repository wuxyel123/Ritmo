# App Store listing copy

Paste-ready text for App Store Connect. Character limits are Apple's and the
counts below were checked against them.

**Two rules worth remembering before editing:**

- Do **not** put Hevy, Strava or OpenPowerlifting in the keywords field. Other
  companies' trademarks in keywords are a common rejection reason. Describing
  the integrations in the description is fine.
- Every claim here is one the app can actually keep. Don't add "improve your
  recovery" style promises: this is an analytics app, not a medical one, and
  health claims invite both rejection and complaints.

**Description** (4000 max)
```
Ritmo turns the health data already on your iPhone into something you can train
with. It reads Apple Health and adds the analysis a training log usually
leaves out.

TRAINING LOAD
Session-RPE load with an acute-versus-chronic trend, so you can see when the
last seven days have run ahead of your four-week baseline. Every workout is
compared against your own average for that kind of session — cardio against
cardio, strength against strength.

DAILY SCORE
One number from movement, recovery, nutrition and training, with the breakdown
always visible. Today's recommendation tells you whether the day suits pushing,
going easy or resting, and says which of your numbers it based that on.

SLEEP AND RECOVERY
Sleep scored across duration, deep and REM share, continuity and schedule
consistency, with an interactive hypnogram. A separate recovery score combines
last night's sleep with HRV and resting heart rate. Nights recorded without
stage detail are scored on what was actually measured rather than counted as
zero.

POWERLIFTING
Competition maxes with IPF GL points, split by full meet and single-lift. A meet
calculator that anchors your third attempt at your max and builds the warm-up
ramp downwards from the opener, so the jumps shrink all the way in. An RPE
calculator on the RTS chart.

ENDURANCE
Personal bests per distance with WMA age-grading, Riegel equivalents, and pace
calculators — including expected pace from heart rate, fitted on your own runs.

APPLE WATCH
A companion app and complications for your daily score, rings, steps with
distance and floors, days since your last workout, and the countdown to your
next meet.

OPTIONAL CONNECTIONS
Bring in your full sets and reps from Hevy, your race results from Strava, and
your competition history from OpenPowerlifting. Each is off by default and uses
your own credentials.

PRIVACY
No account, no server, no analytics, no ads. Your data stays in Apple Health and
on your device. Nothing is ever sent to the developer — there is nowhere to send
it to.

Ritmo needs permission to read Apple Health to be useful, and works best if you
already track workouts or sleep. It is a training tool, not a medical device.
```

**What's New — version 1.0**
```
First release.

Training load, daily score, sleep and recovery analysis, powerlifting meet
planning with attempts and warm-ups, endurance personal bests with age-grading,
an Apple Watch app and complications, and optional Hevy, Strava and
OpenPowerlifting connections.

Everything stays on your device.
```

ENDURANCE

PRIVACY
Nessun account, nessun server, nessuna analitica, nessuna pubblicità. I tuoi dati

Ritmo ha bisogno del permesso di leggere Apple Salute per essere utile, e rende
al meglio se già registri allenamenti o sonno. È uno strumento di allenamento,

## Other fields

- **Category**: Health & Fitness (primary). Sports is a defensible secondary
  given the powerlifting and race features.
- **Age rating**: 4+. Nothing in the app triggers a higher rating.
- **Support URL**: the GitHub repository works if you have nothing else.
- **Marketing URL**: optional, leave empty.
- **Copyright**: `2026 Alessandro Discalzi` — note this displays publicly, like
  the seller name.
- **Review notes**: say that Apple Health permissions must be granted for
  anything to appear, that a reviewer's device will legitimately look empty, and
  that Hevy, Strava and OpenPowerlifting are optional and need the reviewer's own
  accounts, so they are not required to evaluate the app.
